Who I Am

I’m currently learning HTML and CSS. This site is one of my practice projects, and it gives me a place to share a little about myself as well as some photos I’ve taken.

I’m interested in how websites are put together and want to get more comfortable building pages from scratch. For now, I’m focusing on the basics: clear HTML, readable CSS, and layouts that work on a phone as well as a computer.

Why I Chose This Layout

I used a simple top navigation and separate sections so visitors can find the introduction or photos without searching through a long page. The same layout also stacks neatly on smaller screens.

Feedback
